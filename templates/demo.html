01. Cart will Follow you!

Simply walk directly in front of the trolley. The integrated vision system automatically pairs with you and follows your path seamlessly down aisles without physical contact.

02. Manual Control Mode

Take manual command at any moment. Your personal phone serves as a localized remote control layout, letting you steer and position the cart exactly where you need it.

03. Smart Product Detection

Ready to Start Shopping? Hold any Product's barcode in front of the scanner mounted right near the trolley handle. Products log into your database itemized receipt in real-time.

04. Automated Cart Verification

Every product must be scanned! If an item is dropped into the basket without verification, it will flag the addition and a popup will appear on the display screen.

05. Payment on Dashboard

Skip the old checkout counters completely. Finalize transactions securely directly through dashboard using digital payment methods.
